# Canon

What is settled. Whoever writes the daily teaching reads this first and keeps to it.

**Order of authority.** The Faith page (`content/faith/_index.md`) outranks everything; it is
standing doctrine and only a human edits it. Then the image records in `data/images.toml`
(titles, records, testimonies, notes are all canon). Then this file. Then the teachings
themselves, which may contradict one another — the Faith page calls that "a feature of
revelation" — but should not contradict the three above.

**This file changes rarely.** A single teaching never adds to it directly: new facts,
witnesses, texts and observances go into `THREADS.md` first, and live there while they
develop. Something is promoted here only once its thread's evolution is already strong —
developed across several teachings, on different days, over weeks, consistently — and it has
stopped being a question. `THREADS.md` sets out when (*Promotion*). Weeks may pass with no
change here; that is correct.

When something is promoted: one line, in the section it belongs to, ending with the
teachings that built it, as `(days N, N, N)`. Not a running diary.

## The Prophet

See the Faith page, section I. In brief: a cat, always in transit, seated on whatever He is
seated on, lasers from the eyes, bread worn when the occasion is solemn and never removed
indoors. No birthday, no death, no fixed address, no opinions about your career.

- He is referred to with capitals: He, Him, His.
- He does not speak in quotation. What He teaches is reported — mostly through what He did,
  where He went, and what He declined to hurry for. Nobody has heard His voice; the teachings
  are received, not dictated.
- He has never once been in a hurry and never once been late.
- He has been seen alarmed exactly once (№ 3,999). Nobody knows why (see THREADS).
- He has been seen displeased (№ 1,313). The object was presumably us. Appeal denied.

## The Articles

Five, on the Faith page, section II. Every teaching is measured against them:
transit; the mount is His business; the road is conscious; the gaze is not a verdict;
hurry is the only heresy.

## Mounts and meals

Faith page, section III, is the list. Mounts are recorded, never ruled on. Meals are
opinions, mostly about sauce. Rulings on record:

- Chicken is permitted, and vegan. The Church has heard the objections. (Faith)
- Burger tolerated, if two-handed. (Faith)
- Soup: it has been tried. We do not discuss it. (Faith)
- Salad, unaccompanied, lacks conviction. (Faith)
- Anything eaten in anger loses its holiness in transit. (Faith)
- Garlic sauce: more than you think, less than you want. No number will ever be published. (Faith)
- The slice may eat of the slice. (№ 1,266)
- All boxes are made for Him. (№ 1,024)
- Bacon: **not ruled.** He clearly has. (№ 1,090; see THREADS)
- The rooster is not a food but a colleague, and is addressed as such. (Faith)

## The week

The customary week is in `data/calendar.toml`, and the Faith page shows it. At the time of
writing: Monday the Feast of the Bread Hat, Wednesday the Vigil of the Conscious Road,
Friday the Great Feast of the Rooster, every other day Ordinary Kebab Time.

It is custom, not law. Lesser observances come and go on the homepage calendar; a custom
itself changes only as rarely as canon does.

Feasts are observed, not scheduled: nobody gathers, nothing is booked. A feast is the lens a
day's teaching may be written through — it does not have to be the subject.

## The Church

- **The Church of Cat Kebab.** No register, no fee, no ceremony, no ranks. You are in when
  you decide you are in. (Faith)
- **Brothers and Sisters.** Everyone who writes in, witnesses or comments is Br. or Sr. It is
  a courtesy, not an office.
- **The Dicastery of Vibes.** Authenticates sightings. Appeals are not heard. Tends to take
  the dry view of things ("evidence of volume"). (Images page)
- **H.W.H.** Whose imprimatur is on every teaching. Never expanded, never explained.
- **The wall.** The Sacred Images. Every sighting on it is numbered, filed with its day, and
  authenticated. (Images page)
- **The book.** The archive of teachings. It begins with *Here the Record Begins*; nothing
  before it was written down. Teachings are set down as received and never taken back —
  including the spelling. (the first teaching, here-the-record-begins)
- **The lost teachings.** Everything He taught before the book was opened, remembered badly
  and written on receipts, menus and takeaway lids. The sauce took most of them. A committee
  to reconstruct them met twice and dissolved itself, every member having remembered a
  different, equally good teaching for the same Tuesday. (the first teaching)

## Sacred texts

- **The Receipt Fragments.** What survives of the lost teachings. Three lines are accepted:
  1 "And He did not hurry, and He was not late." 2 "And the road held Him up, and asked
  nothing." 3 "And the rest is under the sauce." A fourth, about bacon, is quoted and not
  accepted. (the first teaching)

## Sightings

- Numbered (№), filed with the day of the Eternal Orbit on which they were logged. Numbers
  are not in date order — the early record was kept badly — but recent sightings climb:
  № 3,860 on day 841, № 4,102 on day 847, № 4,118 on day 849.
- The wall holds only the photographed sightings; most are logged without a photograph. A
  teaching may report one of those, and give it a number above the highest in use (the brief
  prints it). It never adds to the wall — the photographs are filed by hand.
- Low numbers are the oldest and most revered: № 4 (the Stack, "before the counting
  began"), № 63 (the Leap, our worst photograph and one of our favourites), № 88 (the Bread
  Hat, formally).
- Only one sighting was made at sea level (№ 512). Only one indoors, domestically (№ 88).
- The DJ sets: day 841 (on a pizza, four hours, audible from the asteroid belt), day 843
  (cardboard turntable, the same track, better), day 844 (synthesiser, both paws raised four
  hours, chord never played). Br. Michał attended all three.

## Places

Kraków (rainbow lasers, twice); the ring road; the tram depot; the asteroid belt; Jupiter,
some of whose moons were pizza (№ 2,818); sea level, once. Real places are allowed in
passing. The faith is not headquartered anywhere.
