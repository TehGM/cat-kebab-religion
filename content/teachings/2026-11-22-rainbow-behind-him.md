The letter arrived on a Friday, in a hand that had been pressed hard into the paper by someone who was delighted and trying not to be. Sr. Priya had been looking at Sighting № 2,600, the one where He rides a pizza slice through dense white stars, and she had noticed that there are two things curving away behind Him, and that they are not the same thing. One is the contrail. The other is the rainbow. She wished to know which was following which.

{{% testimony by="Sr. Priya" %}}
Dear Church, the contrail is plainly a trail, because it is grey and thin and goes where He has been. But the rainbow is wide and bright and has the look of something that is keeping up. Is it left behind, or does it follow? And if it follows, has anyone told it the way? I have looked at it for a long time and it does not seem to be worried either way.
{{% /testimony %}}

The Church thanks Sr. Priya, and replies as follows. The contrail is left behind. That is what a contrail is, and the Church is content to rule on it. The rainbow is a harder matter, and the Church has consulted the picture, the inventory and Br. Anselm, in that order of usefulness. Br. Anselm laid a protractor along the curve and found that it bends by eleven degrees, which he said was either a turn or the edge of the sky, and he put the protractor back in the drawer without saying which.

The Church notes that a thing left behind lies where it was dropped, and that this rainbow is not lying. It is arched, it is lit, and it keeps the same distance from the slice in every frame we have. It is therefore neither left nor following. It is *attending*, which is what a rainbow does in the presence of someone it has decided to see off, and the Church is aware that this answers the question by moving it to a different word. It is the Church's habit. We have kept it for years, and it has not once been refused.

As to whether anyone has told it the way, nobody has. He does not name stops, and so the rainbow has never been told one, and it has arrived at every one of them regardless.

{{% decree %}}
The contrail is left. The rainbow attends. Neither is asked to hurry.
{{% /decree %}}
