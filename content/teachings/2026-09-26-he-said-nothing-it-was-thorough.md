Day one hundred left no mount in the log and no witness to what came directly before it — only a look, already finished by the time anyone raised a camera, aimed with total confidence at everything the Church had built so far. It did not last long. It did not need to.

{{% testimony no="1,313" by="Br. Gordon" %}}
He looked at everything we had built. He said nothing. It was thorough.
{{% /testimony %}}

The file names the object as "us, presumably," which is as close to a defendant as the record gets, and it closes with one further word: denied. What the appeal argued has not survived; only that it failed, and that in every season since, nobody has troubled the file with a second one.

It is kept, we are told, as a warning — against what, the record does not say, and the Church, for once, has not asked. Most sightings are filed for what they show. This one earns its place for what it withholds, which the Church has come to think is the more useful kind.
