Nobody has asked what was in the box before He sat in it, and the Church has decided that nothing was. The box was waiting. Sighting № 1,024 shows it open at the top, worn over His shoulders like armour, the flaps standing out on either side like wings, His chin raised in a way the Church has called proud and has not been contradicted on.

It has been held for years and canonised never, which is an oversight, and it is put right today. The grounds are three. The box has carried Him, upright, at a speed the Church cannot give. It has not once been described as a stopgap, a makeshift or a temporary arrangement, though it is all three on paper. And it bears shipping marks, which are the marks of a thing that was sent somewhere on purpose and has not yet arrived. The Church knows of no sounder label for an object to wear.

The marks have never been read in full. The one legible line says THIS WAY UP, and in the photograph He is, entirely.

{{% pullquote %}}It was sent somewhere, and it is on its way.{{% /pullquote %}}

Br. Callum has asked whether this makes every box holy, and the Church has said that it makes this one holy, and that the others are welcome to be sat in.

{{< decree >}}
The box is a vessel, flaps out. It is not to be flattened, recycled or described as packaging in the hearing of those inside it.
{{< /decree >}}

The tape across the top is not discussed. It is kept, as the tape of anything that was meant to arrive is kept, by being left on.
