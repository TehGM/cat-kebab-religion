Everyone who has watched the loop for its full length has looked away before it ended. The Church has asked around, and the record is unanimous, and the longest anyone lasted is the third pass.

He stands upright on the burrito, amidships, facing the camera dead-on. The starfield is teal and the nebula behind Him is burning orange, and neither of these is the cause. It is that He is looking at the one looking, and has no other plans for the afternoon. There is no laser, no frown and no hurry. He is simply forward, all of it.

{{% testimony no="777" by="Sr. Priya" %}}
I made it to the third pass. On the fourth I looked at my tea. The tea did not look back, and I have thought well of it since.
{{% /testimony %}}

The Church wishes to be clear that this is not a challenge. The gaze is not a verdict, and a thing that is not a verdict cannot be lost. To look away is to answer, politely, that one has received it. Those who stared on, hoping to be the first to outlast Him, have mostly been found afterwards looking at a wall with great concentration.

{{% commentary %}}
**On who blinked first —** The camera. *(Br. Tomasz)*

**On who blinked second —** Not recorded, as He does not. *(Br. Wei, reporting fairly)*
{{% /commentary %}}

The reading-room stool, we note in passing, faces away from № 777. Nobody ruled this. It has simply been how the stool is left, and nobody has had the nerve to turn it.
