In the year of the long queue, the Proud Seat was permitted. Once. By vote. The Church records this not because it is edifying but because concealing it would be worse.

{{< scripture cite="From the Book of Errors, I" >}}
1 | And they sat high, and were pleased with the sitting.
2 | And the weight was added, and the orbit was not achieved.
3 | And He said nothing, which was the whole of the rebuke.
{{< /scripture >}}

The results are still in orbit. We can see them on clear nights, and we have stopped pointing them out to visitors.

{{% commentary %}}
**On the vote —** it carried by one. The one has never been identified and has never come forward. *(disputed, at length)*
{{% /commentary %}}
