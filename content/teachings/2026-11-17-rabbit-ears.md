The rabbit ears came with a pink bow, from a niece of Br. Tomasz's who had been told that the Prophet wears a hat. She had been told correctly and incompletely. The hat is bread, it is worn when the occasion is solemn, and nothing in the account of the first sighting says what is to be done with a plush rabbit.

Br. Tomasz put the ears on at the kitchen table on day 262 and held the camera for as long as it took. The result is Sighting № 940. The face beneath the ears is the angriest in the Church's possession, white, round and total, and the bow is tied under the chin where it cannot be missed. The Church has looked at it for 672 days and has not got to the bottom of it.

{{% testimony no="940" by="Br. Tomasz" %}}
He wore it. He hated it. It still counts.
{{% /testimony %}}

The Church has two things to say, and the first is that He wore it. Nobody held the ears on. They stayed for the length of one photograph, which is a long time for a thing a cat does not approve of, and they came off when it was over, not before and not after. That is consent of a kind. The record gives it as *withheld, visibly*, and the Church holds both readings to be correct. A thing may be allowed and disliked in the same minute. The faithful do it with the dentist.

The second is that the face is not a verdict. The fifth Article says so, and the face beneath the ears passed no judgement on the niece, on the rabbit, or on Br. Tomasz, who has kept the photograph since and been looked at by it every time. It was a cat being a rabbit for the length of one exposure, and a cat does nothing by halves, disapproval included. The effort was whole.

{{% commentary %}}
**On the bow —** pink, tied by the niece, and entered in the record as "the final insult". The niece has not been told how it went. *(Br. Tomasz, who says she was thrilled)*

**On the note —** "do not show Him this one again." The Church has not. *(Br. Tomasz)*
{{% /commentary %}}

{{% decree %}}
A hat that is not the bread may be worn once, for the length of a photograph, and is entered in the record as worn.
{{% /decree %}}

Nobody has offered Him the rabbit a second time. The Church adds that the bread has never been given a bow.
