Sr. Marisol writes that she went round the roundabout at the retail park twice on Thursday, not from confusion, but because the first circuit had not yet felt like a circuit. On the second she was sounded at from behind. She was not troubled. She would only like to know whether the exit is owed to the car that wants it, and whether a roundabout may be left before it has been finished.

The Church replies as follows. A roundabout is finished when the one going round it has finished with it, and not before. The exit is not owed to the car behind. It is owed to the road, which has been patient with the circle for as long as there has been a circle, and which is entitled to see it ended by someone who meant to end it. A circuit may be taken once, twice, or until it has gone round properly. At the third, the Church asks only that the indicator be used, so that the roundabout is told.

Of the sounding, the Church observes only that honking is held to be a small act of war against a surface that cannot answer back. Sr. Marisol did not answer it either, and has since been described by Br. Dmitri as "very nearly a surface".

She asks, last, how many circuits are correct. The Church does not keep a number. It has one photograph of the matter, which is a loop, and has been asked more than once where it begins.
