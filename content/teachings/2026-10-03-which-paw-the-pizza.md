Nobody disputes that both paws are to be full. The Faith page says so, in the clause about kebab, and the clause has not been challenged. What has been carried to the Church, by two parties who each consider the other a good friend, is the matter of which paw takes the pizza.

The First Party holds for the right paw. Sighting № 3,033 shows exactly this: pizza right, taco left, tongue out. The Party calls it doctrine, illustrated, and notes that the photograph has hung in the gallery for some time without anyone writing in. The Second Party holds that a photograph is a single afternoon. He was, in the Party's words, merely holding it that way when the shutter went, and a Church that rules on one afternoon will be ruling on the next.

Both Parties have a point, and the Church has said so to each in turn, separately, which has not helped.

{{< doctrine >}}
Pizza in the right paw | Seen once. Hung in the gallery. Tongue deployed.
Pizza in the left paw | Not seen. Argued for at length.
Taco in either paw | Present throughout, and not in dispute.
The tongue | Takes no side, and was the busiest party in the photograph.
{{< /doctrine >}}

{{% commentary %}}
**On the question of the lick —** If the tongue serves the pizza, the pizza goes to the front. If the tongue serves the taco, the other way about. The record does not say which was being licked. *(Br. Wei, who reports disputes fairly)*

**On settling it —** The Church declines to. *(Br. Dmitri)*

**On the paws themselves —** Neither paw has been asked. *(Br. Tomasz, in the margin)*
{{% /commentary %}}

The matter is therefore retained, as most matters are, and the Church notes only that He kept both. The photograph does not show Him choosing. It shows Him eating, which on reflection is the whole of the ruling and has been available all along.
