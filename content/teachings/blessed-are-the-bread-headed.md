The loaf upon His head is not a crown, and those who describe it as one have misread the entire arrangement. A crown is given. A loaf is picked up, every morning, by the one who intends to wear it.

It is not comfortable. It was never claimed to be comfortable. It shifts on corners and it has been lost twice, both times over water, and both times replaced before the following dawn without comment.

Dignity, we have concluded, is largely a question of balance. The bread-headed walk carefully not because they are proud but because they are holding something, and that is nearly the same thing seen from the correct angle.
