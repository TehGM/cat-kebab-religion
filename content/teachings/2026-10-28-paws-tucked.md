Sighting № 808 shows Him sitting on a peanut butter and jam sandwich with His paws tucked under, so that He is a single brown shape with ears. The Church receives the same few questions about it every month, and has decided to answer them once, in order, so that they can be pointed to.

**Where have the paws gone?**
Under. They are neither lost nor removed. The Church has this on the authority of the faithful's own cats, each of which does it at breakfast.

**Are there four?**
Br. Anselm has counted the paws on № 808 and found four, by working out from the ones that are not there. He asks that this be recorded as a count. The Church records it as a count, with a footnote.

**Is it a posture or a position?**
A posture is held. A position is taken. The loaf is neither, since He is not holding it and has not taken it up: He was going past, and it happened to be the shape He was in. The Church calls it a settling, and notes that the sandwich is still moving.

**Why the sandwich?**
There was one. The two breads are present and neither is worn, which is the correct arrangement for a loaf sitting on bread. The Church sees no need to say more than that a loaf on a loaf is a thing that has been done before.

**May the faithful do likewise?**
They already do. The Church has no objection to a person sitting down with their feet out of sight beneath them for as long as a kettle takes, and holds that this is as far as a person can go.

**How long does it last?**
Until it is over. It has not been timed, because the timing is the one thing He is not in a hurry about.

**So is the loaf stopped?**
No. The loaf is a journey with the paws put away.
