The cat on the wall looked at Sr. Halina, and Sr. Halina looked back, and she did not stop. She had been told that a cat is honoured by attention. She gave it a minute and ten seconds of attention, unblinking, with her whole face, and at the end of the minute the cat turned its head and studied a leaf with great interest. The leaf had done nothing to deserve this.

She was hurt, and then she was informed. The Church wishes it understood that she did nothing wicked. She did something loud. A stare is the gaze with the lasers on, and the lasers are His; Sighting № 1,875 shows what that looks like at full strength, from the front, with six beams and no apology, and He is the only one in the Church's records who has ever been allowed to do it. Everyone else is standing on a pavement.

So she stood still, and she let her eyes close, slowly, as if the lids were being lowered on a cord, and held them there for about the length of a held breath. She opened them again. The cat, who was still on the wall and had not moved, let its own eyes close, and opened them.

Nobody wrote down who blinked first. It has not been asked since the afternoon it was last asked. The leaf was not consulted either.

The Church asks that when you are looked at by a cat, you lower the lasers and answer in kind. It is the same look. It is only quieter, and it can be sent over a wall.
