Sr. Lucia has written about Sighting № 2,100, which she visited on a weekday and which did not wake. She was in front of it for nine minutes, by the clock above the reading room door, and in all nine she spoke only in a whisper, including to the man who came to mop. Her letter is printed here in full.

> *Dear Church, He is asleep in the bucket. I know that a photograph cannot be woken, and I know that the loop, if it were a loop, would not change. But I whispered, and the man with the mop whispered back, and now I do not know whether we did right or only did something. Is the whispering owed? Yours, with a cough I held in, Lucia.*

The Church thanks Sr. Lucia for the cough, which was a good one to hold, and answers her in order.

The whispering was not owed. He is asleep, and has been since day 690, and the sleep is not of a kind that a voice reaches. It is the quietest He has ever been, and the Church has noted that He was quiet already. Nobody has ever been loud at Him and been the worse for it.

The whispering was not wrong either. It was a manner, and manners are for the one who keeps them, and she came away from the bucket having kept one. The man with the mop whispered back because she had, which the Church counts as the sighting's whole effect, and a good one.

The Church asks only that she not whisper at the bucket itself. The bucket is awake and has been the whole time, and it does not wish to be spoken down to.
