A dispute has been carried to Us, at length and with feeling, concerning whether the burrito may be ridden in place of the kebab. It may. We had assumed this was obvious and are told it was not.

{{% marginnote note="three years. three. — Br. Tomasz" %}}
The argument turned, as these arguments always turn, on the question of whether the wrapping constitutes a hull or merely a suggestion. The Church holds that this distinction has never once mattered in flight.
{{% /marginnote %}}

Two conditions attach. The posture must be humble — knees low, tail level, sauce contained. And the destination is not to be announced beforehand, which has less to do with doctrine than with how tiresome it becomes.

{{% decree %}}A burrito ridden humbly travels further than a kebab ridden in pride.{{% /decree %}}
