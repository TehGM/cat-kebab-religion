The kitten stands on the llama, the llama stands on the turtle, and the turtle lies on a burrito, and the Church has been asked for years, which of them is doing the work. The question is a fair one. It is also put by people who have looked at the bottom of the picture and the top of it and have not looked at the middle, where the turtle is.

Two positions are held, and both are held politely.

{{< doctrine >}}
The Party of the Foundation | The burrito carries all of it. It is lowest, it is wrapped, and it has the most to lose if the tiers above it shift. The turtle is a floor and the llama is a floor, and a floor is not a carrier.
The Party of the Passengers | Every tier carries the one above and is carried by the one below. The turtle is therefore the most carried and the most carrying, and the kitten, who carries nothing, is the only one with a free paw.
{{< /doctrine >}}

The Church notes that neither party can say where the weight goes, and that the rainbow, which arcs over all four tiers and rests on none, is the only structure in the picture that nobody has asked to be excused.

{{% commentary %}}
**On the turtle —** I counted four. My sister counted four. The turtle, we assume, counted three. *(Sr. Yuki, the elder of two)*

**On the burrito —** It is the mount. The other three are a view. *(Br. Dmitri, in the voice of the Foundation, and then in the voice of the other party)*

**On the kitten —** Standing, and not a hair of hurry. *(Br. Tomasz)*
{{% /commentary %}}

The Church declines to rank the tiers. The mount is His business, and in this picture it is not even His; it is a kitten's, and the kitten has not said. It rules only that the question be asked of the whole stack at once, kindly, and not of the burrito on its own.
