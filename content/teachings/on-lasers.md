A warned revelation is merely a schedule, and nobody has ever been improved by a schedule.

The lasers arrive when they arrive. They fall on the worthy and the unworthy alike, on people mid-sentence and people asleep, and the Church has established no pattern despite three years of trying reasonably hard.

{{% pullquote %}}Being looked at is the whole of it. It is meant to be enjoyed rather than interpreted.{{% /pullquote %}}

There was a period during which the colours were catalogued. The catalogue is still held, in a drawer, and is not consulted. Green was thought to mean patience until it fell on someone notably impatient, twice, in the same week.

**On the singeing —** it fades. Everyone asks. *(Sr. Halina, night shift)*
