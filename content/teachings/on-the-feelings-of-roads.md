Venerable brothers and sisters: it has reached Our ears that certain of the faithful still honk. They honk at the slow, at the merging, at the merely hesitant. They honk while sitting upon a surface that has held them up every day of their lives without once complaining.

Let them instead consider the motorway. It endures all things. It hopes all things. It is resurfaced approximately never, and it has still not said a word about it. If you must make a noise at something this morning, aim it downward and make it a compliment.

{{% pullquote %}}The road carries you without asking who you are. Try to be worth carrying.{{% /pullquote %}}

{{< part num="I" title="Of the Honking" >}}

We are told the horn was invented for warning. We observe that it is used chiefly for opinion. A warning is a gift; an opinion, at that volume, is a small act of war against a surface that cannot answer back.

{{< scripture cite="From the Book of Wraps, IV" >}}
11 | And He came to the roundabout, and did not signal, for He was already known.
12 | And the asphalt was warm, and said nothing, which is how the patient speak.
13 | And He laid down one kebab upon it, and it was enough.
{{< /scripture >}}

Brothers and sisters, the lesson is not that noise is forbidden. Purring is noise. Choirs are noise. The lesson is that the noise should be addressed to someone who might enjoy receiving it.

{{% testimony image="Screenshot_51.png" no="4,103" by="Sr. Halina, night shift" %}}
He passed over the tram depot at some speed, holding what I believe was a doner. He looked at me. I have not been rude to a road since.
{{% /testimony %}}

As to the second matter. A dispute has been carried to Us concerning vehicles, and whether the burrito may be ridden in place of the kebab. It may. But the posture must be humble: knees low, tail level, sauce contained. Pride adds weight, and weight is the enemy of orbit.

{{< part num="II" title="Of the Posture" >}}

{{% marginnote note="tried the second one. do not recommend. — Br. Tomasz" %}}
There are three postures, and the faithful should know them by name, because two of them will get you dropped somewhere near Jupiter and only one will bring you home in time for supper.
{{% /marginnote %}}

{{< doctrine >}}
The Low Seat | Knees beneath the shoulders, tail level, gaze forward. Correct, and dull, which is how correctness usually feels.
The Proud Seat | Chest out, chin high, one paw raised in salute. Adds weight. Weight is the enemy of orbit. Do not.
The Sauce-Forward Seat | Permitted only to those who have already lost a wrap in flight and learned from it. Most of us have not.
{{< /doctrine >}}

{{< litany label="Said together, before setting out" >}}
Cat upon the kebab | ride for us.
Rooster of the void | carry us.
Bread upon the brow | steady us.
Patient asphalt | forgive our tyres.
{{< /litany >}}

{{< practice
  do="Cross one car park slowly. Notice what holds you up. Compliment it aloud if nobody is watching, internally if they are."
  avoid="Honking. Revving. Standing on a kerb while speaking ill of that same kerb. Riding any food you have not thanked." >}}

Go in peace, and go slowly. He rides ahead of you regardless, and He has never once been in a hurry.

{{% decree %}}The burrito is a licit vehicle. The horn is not a sacrament.{{% /decree %}}

{{% commentary %}}
**On "worth carrying" —** some read this as a burden. It is not. A road does not weigh you; it merely remembers. *(Br. Michał, disputed)*

**On the second posture —** the Proud Seat was permitted once, in the year of the long queue, and the results are still in orbit.
{{% /commentary %}}
