More than you think. Less than you want.

The faithful have asked, repeatedly, for a number. There is no number. The Prophet has never once measured it and has never once been wrong, and the Church regards these two facts as the same fact stated twice.

{{< doctrine >}}
Too little | The wrap survives the journey and nobody remembers it.
Sufficient | Some escapes. This is not failure; this is the evidence.
Too much | You have made the sauce the point, and the sauce was never the point.
{{< /doctrine >}}

{{% decree %}}No quantity shall be published. Those who require a number have already erred.{{% /decree %}}
