It goes around. That is the entire case for it, and the Church considers the case closed.

A roundabout arrives nowhere. It has no far end to be reached and no progress to report. Every vehicle upon it is, briefly, going in a direction it did not intend, and every one of them leaves in better order than it came.

{{% pullquote %}}It goes around for us all, without complaint and without arrival.{{% /pullquote %}}

He declared it holy on a Tuesday, which is the least ceremonial day available, and then circled it eleven times. Witnesses disagree on whether this was devotion or difficulty leaving. The Church has ruled that the distinction does not arise.

**On the eleven circuits —** the number is not significant. We have checked. *(Sr. Aniela)*
