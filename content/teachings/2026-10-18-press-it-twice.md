The button at the crossing outside the tram depot has been pressed once, and its small orange light has come on, and that is the whole of the transaction. The Church wishes it understood that the light means the request was received. It does not mean the light is hurrying.

Some of the faithful press again. The Church has looked into this at length and finds that the second press hastens nothing, delays nothing and offends nobody, least of all the button, which feels both and cannot tell them apart. It is not forbidden. It is a vigil: the press made while waiting, so that the hand has something to do and the rest of the person may stay where it is.

Sr. Yuki presses it twice and her younger sister three times. They cross on the same green. The Dicastery has recorded this and drawn no conclusion, which is its usual conclusion, and the plate on the button has been worn smooth at the one spot, which is the nearest thing to a verdict the crossing has given.

{{< practice
  do="Press the button once. Wait. Press it a second time, lightly, as one confirms a thing already understood. Then stand as if you had pressed it only once."
  avoid="A third press made in a tone of voice." >}}

{{% commentary %}}
**On the second press —** It is not for the green. It is for the hand. *(Br. Tomasz)*
**On the third —** Counted, not condemned. *(Br. Michał, disputed)*
{{% /commentary %}}

The Church observes that the light was lit the moment the first press landed, and that nothing the faithful do afterwards makes it any more lit. The Prophet has been seen at no crossing whatever, and is understood to regard the matter as settled.
