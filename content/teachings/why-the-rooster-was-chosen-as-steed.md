The rooster cannot fly. This is the first thing anyone says about him, usually with a small laugh, and it is the first thing the Prophet noticed too — though He did not laugh, and He did not consider it a defect.

A creature that cannot fly and is asked to carry you through open space must proceed on some other principle entirely. Nobody has established what that principle is. The rooster declines to discuss it.

{{% pullquote %}}He was chosen not despite the objections, but because he makes them out loud.{{% /pullquote %}}

He cannot hurry, which we have already established is the whole of the faith. And he cannot be reasoned with, which means every journey taken upon him is a negotiation that you will lose, slowly, in front of witnesses.

{{% decree %}}The rooster is a colleague, not a mount. He is to be addressed as such.{{% /decree %}}
