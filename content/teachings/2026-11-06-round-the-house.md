At three in the morning something went down the hall at the speed of a dropped tray, turned left through the kitchen without slowing, and came back along the other side of the house by way of the sofa. That is the whole of the letter's first paragraph, and the Church has read it four times.

{{% testimony by="Br. Michał" %}}
I was awake, as I am. It did the hall, the kitchen and the sofa in under nine seconds. Nothing was chasing it and nothing was ahead of it. I wish to ask what the lap was for, because I would like to be able to say, and because I have now been woken by it, and was already awake.
{{% /testimony %}}

The Church thanks Br. Michał and begins by correcting him. It was not a lap. A lap returns to where it began, and the faithful will notice that nothing in this account returns. He passed through the hall, the kitchen and the sofa the way He passes through a nebula, at the speed the nebula allows, which at that hour is a great deal. Sighting № 1,660 shows the same thing from above: head down, lasers forward, a rainbow behind, and a moon coming up whose opinion was not sought.

The hour is not explained either, and the Church declines to. It notes only that the house is small, that He is in transit, and that at three o'clock the transit has the whole of it to itself. Br. Michał asks what it was for. The Church replies that Br. Michał asks this of everything, and that it is the reason he is awake.

The hall carpet has a worn line down the middle which the household has always put down to the post. The Church makes no finding.

He was asleep on the sofa by ten past, one paw over the edge. Br. Michał sat up with the sofa until it was light, and records that nothing else happened, which is the correct amount.
