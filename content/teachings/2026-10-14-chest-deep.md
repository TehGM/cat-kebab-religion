Venerable brothers and sisters, there is a kitten in the open end of a burrito, and it is neither in nor out. The Church has held Sighting № 1,961 for some years and has never once been asked which way the kitten is going. We have been asked, regularly, why it has not finished.

It is chest-deep. Two paws are on the tortilla and the rest is somewhere warm. Its eyes are wide, and not from alarm: the Church has checked, and the one sighting of alarm is a different cat in a different frame. This is the look of a creature that has found the middle of something and sees no fault in it. Two blue beams pass behind, unaddressed. The kitten lets them pass.

We are taught to value the arrival, and the Faith values the transit. The middle is neglected by both. It has no door and no view, and nobody sends a card to it. Yet most of any journey is spent there, and the faithful who dislike the middle dislike most of their lives.

{{% pullquote %}}Chest-deep is a place, and it may be stayed in.{{% /pullquote %}}

Let it be said, since it was asked, that the burrito is not a hull. Whether it is anything more than a suggestion has been disputed for years and has never mattered in flight. The kitten has not weighed in. It is busy being exactly as far out as it is.
