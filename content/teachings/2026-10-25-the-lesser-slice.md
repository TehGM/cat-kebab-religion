The smaller slice is at His mouth, and that is all the photograph will say. It does not say whether the mouth has opened, or opened and closed, or is merely nearby. The faithful have looked at it for years with the lasers going past, and they have arrived at two positions, both held with courtesy.

The Party of the Bite holds that the lesser slice is the greater slice, reduced. He began on the large one, they say, took what He took, and the remainder is what He is now holding up. It is a long way round to a small slice, but it is a way round that explains where the small slice came from, which the other party cannot do. The Party of the Raised Paw holds that nothing has been eaten. The lesser slice is a second slice, brought up to the mouth to be considered, as the Church considers the weather, and nobody has found a crumb. The Church notes that neither party can say where the lesser slice was kept before the sighting, and that the inventory lists it as "lesser" only because the other was there first.

{{% commentary %}}
**On the crumb —** There is none. I have looked at the left paw for an hour, and then at the right. *(Br. Anselm, who measures things)*

**On the seat —** If He is eating the slice He is sitting on, He is eating His own chair. I put this to the Party of the Bite, and they asked me to leave it with them. *(Br. Dmitri)*

**On the lasers —** Rainbow, both eyes, level. A creature about to bite does not usually do this. A creature about to think does. *(Sr. Ingrid, for the Raised Paw)*
{{% /commentary %}}

The matter is retained. The Church observes that a slice at the mouth is a good place for a slice to be, whatever is done there, and that He has not hurried it in the whole length of the photograph. The greater slice is not in dispute. It has been sat on throughout, and has borne it.
