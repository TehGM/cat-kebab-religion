**Q. Is the red man waited for, or waited at?**
A. Waited for. To wait at him is to stand facing a small illuminated figure and hope it feels watched. He has never once felt watched.

**Q. May one cross against him on an empty road?**
A. The road is not empty. It is conscious. The Church has asked it and it says it is not at all empty, and would like to be consulted.

**Q. How long is the wait?**
A. As long as it is. Br. Anselm has timed it at forty-one seconds, at ninety, and once at four minutes, and holds all three correct.

**Q. What is one to do while waiting?**
A. Stand behind the kerb with the toes at the edge, and look at nothing in particular, slowly. The Prophet has been seen to recline for the whole of one. That is permitted, though rarely practicable in a coat.

**Q. And when the green man comes?**
A. He is not to be thanked. He has been on the whole time, in a sense, and is only now being noticed.

**Q. Is the red man ever wrong?**
A. The Church declines to say. Eleven faithful have written in on the point, and each was waiting at the time.
