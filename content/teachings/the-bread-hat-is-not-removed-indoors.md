Manners are for rooms. The loaf is for eternity. Where the two conflict, the Church has found it simplest to side with eternity and apologise afterwards.

He has worn it to three weddings and one tribunal. He was thanked on each occasion, which the faithful cite as vindication and the Dicastery cites as good hosting.
