Twice. That is the whole record, and the whole record is enough.

Those who defend the turtle tend to defend it on the wrong grounds — insisting it is faster than it looks, or that the distance covered was greater than reported. Neither claim is necessary and neither is true.

{{% pullquote %}}He was not in a hurry. The turtle was not in a hurry. Nothing about the arrangement required defending.{{% /pullquote %}}

The turtle suits Him precisely because it concedes the point that every other mount argues. A rooster objects. A pizza rotates. A turtle simply proceeds, and has been proceeding since long before anyone thought to photograph it.
