Sighting № 2,210 shows two cats, each rolled snugly into its own tortilla, drifting through a brown and violet nebula. One is the right way up. The other is not. The Church is asked about this more often than about any other picture on the wall, and has therefore drawn up its answers in one place, so that it need not give them again.

**Which of them is correct?**
The one the right way up is the more comfortable to look at. Correctness does not come into it, and neither wrap has put in a claim.

**Is the upside-down one in distress?**
The Dicastery has examined the face. The face is resigned. Resignation is not distress; it is what a creature looks like when it has understood the arrangement and has decided to carry on with it.

**Should it be turned over?**
By whom? A rota for turning it was drawn up once. Nobody put their name to it, and the sheet is kept in the drawer with the other things nobody has signed.

**Which way is up in a nebula?**
Neither way. A nebula has no up to offer, and the faithful are asked to remember that "upside down" is a remark about the viewer's neck. Br. Dmitri has been asked to settle it, and has declined.

**Is one of them going faster?**
No. Both are drifting at the same rate, in the same direction, at the same unhurried speed, and the Church has checked this against the stars, which agree.

**What is owed to the upside-down one?**
A place in the frame, which it has. What is owed to the other is the same, and it has that too. Between them, the Church has found, the whole account is settled.

**What does the wrap think?**
The wrap has not been asked. It is a tortilla.
