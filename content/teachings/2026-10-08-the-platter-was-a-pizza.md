The platter was a pepperoni pizza, and it turned at the correct speed for the whole of the set. This is the first thing the photograph establishes and, the Church has come to feel, the last thing it can prove. Nobody has been able to say which speed that was.

He stands behind the deck with the tonearm down and one paw resting on the record, not pressing, not scratching, simply keeping it company. The green and purple starfield behind Him is stationary. Everything that moves in the photograph is a pizza.

{{% testimony no="3,860" by="Br. Michał" %}}
Four hours. It did not slow, it did not cool, and nobody asked it to. I was there for all of it and awake for most of that.
{{% /testimony %}}

The deck is covered in stickers. They have not been catalogued, and the Church has resolved, again, to catalogue them once there is a quiet week, which is how the matter has stood since the photograph was hung.

{{% commentary %}}
**On the music —** The record contains none. A pizza makes no sound turning. *(Br. Kofi, who has looked)*

**On the music, again —** Br. Michał heard it from the belt and describes it as "correct". *(disputed)*
{{% /commentary %}}

What the Church can say is that the set was kept, to its end, by a platter that could have been eaten at any point and was not. He does not appear to have considered it. The Church has considered it for some time, and has decided it is a kind of teaching.
