Gravel is asphalt that was passed over for promotion. It knows this. It has made peace with it, in the way that things which cannot leave eventually make peace with everything.

{{< scripture cite="From the Book of Surfaces, II" >}}
4 | And He came upon the lesser stones, and did not go around.
5 | And He purred, and the stones did not answer, for they were listening.
{{< /scripture >}}

The faithful will observe that gravel is walked upon by people hurrying somewhere better. It is the surface of car parks, of verges, of the bit before the actual path begins. Nobody photographs it. Nobody thanks it.

Purr at it anyway. The gravel remembers who did, and the gravel is in no hurry to forget.
