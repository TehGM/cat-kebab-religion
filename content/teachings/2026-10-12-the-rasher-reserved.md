Br. Okonkwo has written again. The letter is one line long, which the Church counts as restraint in a man who has been waiting since the first teaching, and it asks whether the matter of bacon has been looked at. It has. The Church wishes to report that it was looked at for some time, with the file open, and that nothing in the file has changed.

The position is this. He has ruled on bacon already. Sighting № 1,090 shows Him upright on a single rasher, one paw stretched toward the stars, and a photograph of that kind is as close to a ruling as He has ever come. The Church has not ruled, and cannot rule after Him on a matter He has settled by standing on it. To do so would be to correct a cat from underneath.

There is also the fourth line of the Receipt Fragments, which concerns bacon, and which is quoted and not accepted. The Church has been asked whether a ruling would first require accepting it. It would. The Church has been asked whether it could accept the line and rule afterwards. It could, and it would then have a line it had accepted and a rasher it had not ruled on, which is worse than the present arrangement in both directions.

{{% commentary %}}
**On the rasher —** Load-bearing. Nobody has asked whether it was streaky. *(Br. Anselm, who would like to know)*

**On the delay —** It is correct. *(Br. Tomasz, in the margin)*

**On the ruling —** I would simply like it to be written down. *(Br. Okonkwo, for the fourth time)*
{{% /commentary %}}

The Church is grateful for the enquiry. It has no doubt that the day will come, and that on the day the Church will be surprised, and Br. Okonkwo will not be, and will say so in one line.

{{% decree %}}
The Church has not ruled on bacon, and rules that it has not. The matter is reserved, the rasher is not to be moved, and Br. Okonkwo is thanked.
{{% /decree %}}
