The question reached the Church in one word, and the word was "seized". It was put by a visitor who had seen Sighting № 227, in which a grey kitten with blue lasers for eyes reaches out into a purple starfield and has a passenger jet in its paw, and who wished to know what the Church proposed to do about the jet.

The Church has looked at the photograph for some time. The paw is open. The claws are not out. The jet is level, its nose is where a nose should be, and it is going at the speed it was going, which is the speed of everything in the picture except the cat, who is going at none. A thing that is seized is stopped. This one has been accompanied, and the Church would call it a short escort with an unusual grip.

The aeroplane's own view is not recorded. The Dicastery has noted that the picture is of low resolution, and that the window blinds are all down, and has drawn no conclusion from either.

{{% commentary %}}
**On the paw —** Open. I have measured it against the fuselage twice and the fuselage was longer both times. *(Br. Callum, who owns a telescope)*

**On the word —** "Seized" has a sound to it. "Held" does not. That is the whole difference. *(Br. Tomasz, in the margin)*

**On the jet —** It is not enjoying this. *(the Dicastery of Vibes, unsigned and undisputed)*
{{% /commentary %}}

A letter of apology to the airline was drafted when the sighting was filed. It is addressed to the flight, care of the weather, and it is in the drawer, unsent, since nobody has found the right weather.

{{< decree >}}
The aeroplane was held, not seized. It was let go, and landed. The word "seized" is withdrawn from the record, and "held" is entered, in a smaller hand.
{{< /decree >}}
