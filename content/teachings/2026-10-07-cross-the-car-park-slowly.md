A car park is crossed at the speed of the slowest thing in it, and the Church has found that this is usually a trolley with one bad wheel. The faithful are not asked to be slower than that. They are asked not to be faster, which is harder, since the door of the shop is in plain view the whole way and the body wants to arrive.

The first step is the bay. Do not take the nearest one. Take the one the furthest from the door that still has a white line on both sides, and park in it squarely, because the road is conscious and has noticed a bay that is taken crooked. Br. Anselm has paced the distance from the far bay to the door at four hundred and twelve steps. He recommends none of them be hurried.

The second step is the car that waits. Sooner or later one will stop for you, indicator off, engine idling, with the plain patience of a thing that has been told it may go and has decided to be sure. It is owed one nod. Not a wave, which asks it to stay, and not a jog, which asks it to be quick about it. One nod, at walking pace, and the crossing continues as before.

The Church is aware that it has described a walk. It does not apologise. The road has been thanked before at this pace, and sees no reason to change it for a second time.
