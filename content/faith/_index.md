## I · The Prophet

{{% marginfigure image="Screenshot_51.png" alt="The Prophet in orbit" caption="Among the clearer images we hold. [Others are on the wall](/images/)." %}}
He is a cat. He is seated upon a shawarma, or a pizza, or a rooster, or a turtle, or a llama, and on certain days upon nothing at all, and He moves through open space without visible means of propulsion. He does not explain this. We have stopped asking.

From His eyes proceed lasers of every colour, which is how the faithful know a genuine sighting from a trick of the light. He wears bread upon His head when the occasion is solemn, and He has never once removed it indoors.

He has no birthday, no death, no fixed address and no opinions about your career. He appears, He is photographed badly, and He moves on. Sightings are logged as they are reported, numbered, and filed whether or not anyone believes them — we have some thousands, and no method of verification beyond agreement.

What He teaches arrives as a single short text each day, which we publish unedited and never take back. [The archive](/teachings/) holds every one of them in order, including the several that contradict each other. We have decided this is a feature of revelation rather than a fault in the record.

## II · Articles of Faith

Five articles, held since the beginning and unlikely to change. They are what a teaching is measured against; if a daily teaching appears to contradict one of them, the teaching is being dramatic and should be read again in a better mood.

{{< articles >}}
**He is always in transit.** Nobody has seen Him standing still, arriving anywhere, or waiting for anything. Whatever else the faith claims, it begins here: He is moving, and He was moving before we noticed.
**What He rides is His business.** A kebab most often, but also a rooster, a turtle, a llama, and on some occasions nothing whatsoever. The mount is never the point; mistaking it for the point is the commonest error in the faith.
**The road is conscious.** Asphalt feels every footstep and every tyre, remembers all of it, and complains about none of it. It is the only thing in this faith we are not joking about, which is itself a strange position to be in.
**The gaze is not a verdict.** The lasers arrive without warning and fall on the worthy and unworthy alike. Nobody has established what the colours mean, and the Church has quietly stopped trying — being looked at is the whole of it, and it is meant to be enjoyed rather than interpreted.
**Hurry is the only heresy.** He has never once been in a hurry and has never once been late, which the Church regards as settled evidence rather than coincidence.
{{< /articles >}}

## III · Of Mounts and Meals

Two separate questions are often confused. What He rides is a matter of record — we log it, we do not rule on it. What He carries is a matter of appetite, and the Church has opinions there, though it concedes they are only opinions and mostly about sauce.

{{< cols >}}
{{< col title="Mounts, attested" >}}
Shawarma — the oldest attested, and the reason for the name
Pizza — frequent, and the only mount He has been seen DJ-ing from
Burrito — reported since the third year, posture permitting
Rooster — not a food but a colleague, and addressed as such
Turtle — twice, both times unhurried, which suits Him
Llama — once, at considerable altitude, unexplained
Nothing at all — increasingly common; He appears to have stopped needing one
{{< /col >}}
{{< col title="Meals, held licit" >}}
Kebab, in either paw, and preferably in both
Chicken — permitted, and vegan. The Church has ruled on this. The Church is aware of the objections and has heard them all
Burger — tolerated, if two-handed
Bread, worn — not a meal but a crown of sorts
Garlic sauce — more than you think, less than you want
{{< /col >}}
{{< col title="Held doubtful" wide="true" >}}
Soup — it has been tried. We do not discuss it
Salad, unaccompanied — lacks conviction
Anything eaten in anger — loses its holiness in transit

## IV · Feasts & Observances

The week is observed rather than scheduled. Nobody gathers, nothing is booked, and no attendance is taken — a feast is simply the lens the day's teaching is written through, and a reason to eat something specific about it.

{{< feasts >}}
Great Feast of the Rooster | Every Friday. Bring sauce. Do not bring the rooster.
Feast of the Bread Hat | Mondays. A loaf is worn indoors without apology.
Vigil of the Conscious Road | Wednesdays, at walking pace. Say one kind thing downward.
Ordinary Kebab Time | All remaining days. The bulk of the faith, and the best of it.
{{< /feasts >}}

## V · Joining

There is no register, no fee, no ceremony and nobody to inform. There is no membership to lose and no rank to climb. You are in when you decide you are in, and out the moment it stops being fun, which we consider the only honest arrangement.

If you want something to actually do: read the day's teaching, walk somewhere slowly, and greet the pavement — out loud if the street is empty, internally if it is not. That is the entire practice, and it takes eleven seconds. Everything else on this site — the feasts, the seals, the numbered articles, the archive of photographs — is decoration, and we are extremely fond of decoration.

Two habits are discouraged rather than forbidden: hurrying, and honking. Neither has ever improved a journey, and the Prophet has managed without both since the beginning.
